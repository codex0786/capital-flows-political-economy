Theoretical mechanism

An election period bundles three forces

ChannelPrediction for gross inflowsMost exposed flows
Policy uncertainty (wait-and-see)Lower near and before the voteIrreversible FDI
Pre-electoral fiscal policyHigher financing needs may raise debt inflowsPortfolio debt
Post-vote repricingAdjustment after the outcome is knownBank and other investment

Institutions that make policy reversal costly should shrink the uncertainty channel. Reverse causation also runs from capital-flow stress to political crises.

Julio and Yook 2012 and 2016 for the real-options channel; Brender and Drazen 2005 for political budget cycles; Canes-Wrone and Park 2012 show election effects are conditional on polarisation.
