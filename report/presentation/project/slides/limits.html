Identification limits

What these estimates can and cannot say

Supported reading

Scheduled election timing is set years ahead, so the election-period coefficient is plausibly causal for the bundle "election period", not for uncertainty alone.

Not supported

Early elections, governance and policy changes are endogenous: associations only. Quarterly flows are noisy, so effects below ~0.1 SD are undetectable.

Not yet done: UNCTAD greenfield/M&A, BIS banking flows, hand-coded policy events, election closeness, WGI vintage test (only the revised 2026 vintage is available).

The stacked clean-control design addresses TWFE contamination but its standard errors of 3 to 5 pp make it uninformative about effects of 1 to 2 pp.
