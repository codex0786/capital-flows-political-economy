The question

Do elections, institutions and global shocks change which capital flows in or out, and when?

Gap in push–pull

The workhorse capital-flow framework rarely includes dated political events.

A recent IMF result

Private inflows to 38 EMs fell around elections, 1990–2020.

Our test

121 economies, AEs included, 2015–2025, pre-registered before estimation.

Arakelyan and Evdokimova (2025), IMF WP/25/243, is the closest prior study. We read its full text only after our pre-registered estimates were computed, which matters for how we label results later.
